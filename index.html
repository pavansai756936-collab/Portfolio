What's real vs. prototype

The Flask app, six-class API, and dashboard above are fully working — the charts are the actual project data. The CNN itself currently trains on randomly generated image tensors rather than the labeled sample photos, so its 60.5% validation accuracy reflects the training pipeline, not real-world parking recognition yet.

"Training currently runs on synthetic image tensors — next step is retraining on real labeled photos and evaluating with a confusion matrix."
